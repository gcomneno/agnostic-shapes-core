# PETRA Status

## Current architecture

**Agnostic SHAPES Core (ASHES)** is the maintained project container.

**SHAPES** is its interpretation-agnostic structural core.

**PETRA — Prime Exponent Tower Recursive Algebra** is one maintained,
pluggable interpretation layer over SHAPES. It does not own or define the
SHAPES carrier.

Status: **PETRA v2.1.0 released — canonical theory programme complete and
intrinsic runtime implemented**.

## Canonical theory

The normative carrier is:

```text
P ::= Node(M_f(P))
```

where every node has a finite multiset of child forms.

The canonical theory establishes:

- primitive direct parent-child incidence;
- intrinsic child multiplicity;
- no intrinsic sibling order;
- recursive multiset structural equality;
- `Node(empty multiset)` as the unique zero-child form;
- the intrinsic elementary edit algebra generated by `ADD` and `REMOVE`;
- exact intrinsic inverse relation between `ADD` and `REMOVE`;
- separation of carrier, representation, compatibility surfaces, and external
  interpretations.

The complete-theory programme tracked by issue #276 is closed as completed.

## Maintained runtime

The maintained runtime under `src/petra/` exposes the intrinsic API:

- `intrinsic_equal`;
- `intrinsic_size`;
- `intrinsic_add`;
- `intrinsic_remove`.

The existing typed runtime model remains maintained as a representation and
compatibility layer:

- `Leaf`;
- `Container`;
- `Term`;
- `Root`;
- positional addresses;
- invocation/result serialization;
- typed results and witnesses;
- `SPROUT`;
- `SHED`;
- `GRAFT`;
- `PRUNE`;
- the `petra` console command.

The four named public operators preserve their established targeting and
result/witness contracts while delegating their structural effects to the
intrinsic `ADD`/`REMOVE` layer.

## Interpretation boundary

PETRA is defined structurally.

Prime assignment, exponent terminology, numeric projection, primality testing,
prime generation, and integer factorization are not prerequisites of the
intrinsic carrier or edit algebra. They may exist only as derived
interpretations or external layers.

The dependency direction is:

```text
interpretation depends on PETRA
PETRA does not depend on interpretation
```

## Satellite projects

`resolver/` remains a derived research satellite. It imports from PETRA and
does not define PETRA semantics.

Resolver has its own package metadata and is not automatically version-bumped
with PETRA. Its current package version remains `2.0.0`.

## Historical runtime

The former `src/pet/` runtime and historical PET test/tool surface were removed
during Phase 10. They remain recoverable through repository history and tag
`v0.3.0`.

PETRA does not depend on any historical PET runtime surface.

## Research boundary

Research material under `docs/research/` remains non-normative unless promoted
through an explicit canonical decision and specification change.

The completed research programme produced the canonical carrier, minimal
algebra, interpretation boundary, meta-theory investigations, external
mathematical validation, and the subsequent normative promotion now reflected
in the specification.
